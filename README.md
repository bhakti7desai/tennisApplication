# Tennis Analytics 🎾

An AI-powered tennis analytics project focused on tracking player performance, visualizing match statistics, and exploring video-based stroke analysis.

## About

I'm building this project to make tennis performance easier to track and understand. The goal is to bring match statistics, performance trends, and AI-assisted analysis into one place for players and coaches.

I want to go beyond just recording match results and explore how data can help players recognize patterns, understand their game, and identify areas to improve.

## Planned Features

- **Match Statistics:** Track match performance and important player stats.
- **Data Visualization:** Turn match data into charts and performance trends.
- **Player Analytics:** Monitor individual progress over time.
- **Video Analysis:** Explore AI-assisted analysis of serves, forehands, backhands, slices, and other strokes.
- **Team Analytics:** Help coaches understand team performance and player development.

## Motivation

Tennis performance involves more than wins and losses. I want to explore how statistics and AI can help players and coaches better understand what happens on the court and make more informed decisions.

This project is a way for me to combine my interests in tennis, software development, and AI while building something that can be useful to real players.
